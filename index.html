When a place of care falls short

Choosing a nursing home means placing enormous trust in a facility and the people who work there. Residents deserve thoughtful attention, respect, and a safe environment—every day.

Unfortunately, not every facility provides the quality of care families expect and residents deserve. A pattern of missed needs or unsafe treatment may raise serious concerns about neglect or abuse.
