When a place of care falls short

Choosing a nursing home means placing enormous trust in a facility and the people who work there. Residents deserve thoughtful attention, respect, and a safe environment—every day.

Unfortunately, not every facility provides the quality of care families expect and residents deserve. A pattern of missed needs or unsafe treatment may raise serious concerns about neglect or abuse.

New Jersey Abuse Lawyers listens carefully, explains the path ahead in plain language, and helps families seek accountability when something does not feel right.

Know what to look for

Small changes can tell a story

Every situation is different. These concerns may warrant a closer look, especially when they are unexplained, recurring, or left unaddressed.

01

Unexplained injuries

Repeated bruises, falls, fractures, or injuries without a clear and consistent explanation.

02

Changes in wellbeing

Sudden fearfulness, withdrawal, agitation, or other significant changes in mood and behavior.

03

Unmet daily needs

Signs that a resident may not be receiving adequate help with hygiene, mobility, or meals.

04

Medication concerns

Confusing medication changes, missed doses, or care plans that do not seem to be followed.

05

Unsafe conditions

Unsanitary spaces, preventable hazards, or a lack of supervision that puts residents at risk.

06

Repeated unanswered concerns

Questions dismissed or concerns left unresolved by facility staff and management.
